4. Send it to Sparky

For now, faces go into the firmware when you upload it to the ESP32. Wi-Fi updates come later.

Download sparky_faces.h Download voice lines (.zip)
  1. Put sparky_faces.h in the same folder as the Sparky Arduino sketch.
  2. Add #include "sparky_faces.h" at the top of the sketch.
  3. Show a mood with sparkyDrawFace(mx, SPARKY_MOOD_FACES[SPARKY_MOOD_HAPPY]);
  4. Upload to the ESP32. If a face appears mirrored, change SPARKY_FLIP_X at the top of the file.
