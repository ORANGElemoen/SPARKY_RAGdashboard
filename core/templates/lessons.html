Lesson

Sparky offers lessons by title, so keep it short and easy to say.
One exciting sentence to start with.
Each line follows "Today you will learn ...", e.g. "how a lemon can make electricity".
Sparky says one step at a time and waits for "next". Include "do this with a grown-up" where it matters.
Keep answers short (a word or two). Sparky accepts answers that mean the same thing.
